We are football and technology enthusiasts on a single mission — bring objective, data-driven decisions to Indian football. Talent shouldn't depend on who's watching. It should be measured — in speed, distance, acceleration, and effort — every session, every match, every player.

The System

One Tag. One Platform. Total Visibility.

PulseTag pairs a lightweight wearable sensor with a cloud analytics platform — giving players and coaches the same calibre of data used by professional clubs, built for the realities of grassroots football.

Hardware · Wearable

PulseTag Sensor

A 26-gram GPS + IMU tracker worn between the shoulder blades. Captures position, speed, acceleration, and movement patterns at 10Hz — through training and 90-minute matches.

Live Match Feed
Software · Analytics

Pulse Dashboard

Every session synced and processed into player profiles, heatmaps, and development trends — accessible to players, coaches, and academy directors in real time.

The Process

From Pitch to Profile

A simple loop that turns raw movement into actionable insight — built to run every single session, without extra effort from coaches or players.

01

Wear

Slot the PulseTag into the vest pocket before training or matchday. No setup, no syncing required.

02

Play

The sensor records position, speed, and load continuously — completely out of the way of the game.

03

Sync

Data uploads automatically to the Pulse Dashboard the moment the session ends.

04

Improve

Players and coaches review trends, compare benchmarks, and plan the next session with evidence.
